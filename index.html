our story

From a cart on Andriyivskyy to a home that smells of cinnamon

Ten years ago two friends bought an old lever espresso machine and started making coffee right on the street. Today we have our own roastery, a bakery behind the wall and two rooms where you can easily spend the whole morning.

We’re not trying to be the trendiest place in town. We want you to walk in like it’s your own kitchen — for your usual flat white, a warm croissant and a chat with a barista who remembers your name.
