Our story

How a coffee cart grew into a café with its own roastery, a bakery and two rooms — and why we still work behind the bar ourselves.

our oak bar is almost a century old

timeline

Ten years in five chapters

  2. 2016

    A cart on Andriyivskyy Descent

    It all started with a coffee cart, a lever espresso machine and two friends who wanted to make coffee the way they drank it in Vienna and Lisbon.

  3. 2018

    Our first 24 square metres

    We rented a former watchmaker’s workshop in Podil: six stools, a bar made of old oak and a queue round the corner on Saturdays.

  4. 2020

    Roasting our own

    We bought a small 5 kg roaster and started roasting our own beans. On Thursdays the whole block smells of caramel.

  5. 2022

    A bakery next door

    We opened a bakery right behind the wall. Croissants now travel exactly eight steps from the oven to the counter.

  6. 2024

    A second room and a big table

    We expanded next door: a second room with a large oak table for eight and a quiet corner for working.

from bean to cup

Four steps we never skip

  1. 01

    Green beans

    We buy green coffee from importers who work directly with farms in Ethiopia, Colombia and Brazil.

  2. 02

    Roasting

    We roast in small batches on Thursdays and let the beans rest for 48 hours before brewing.

  3. 03

    Extraction

    Every morning we dial in: 18 grams in, 36 grams out, 27 seconds — and taste until it’s right.

  4. 04

    Serving

    We steam milk to 62 °C so it stays sweet. And we never serve a cup we wouldn’t drink ourselves.

what we believe

Three rules that hang above our bar

  1. 1

    Taste comes first

    If the espresso isn’t right today, we dial in the grinder again instead of hoping nobody will notice.

  2. 2

    Nothing from yesterday

    Pastries and desserts live in the case for one day. In the evening, leftovers go to the shelter volunteers next street over.

  3. 3

    Guests are neighbours

    We remember names, your favourite milk and that you don’t like cinnamon. Because that’s what you do at home.

Better to taste it once.
